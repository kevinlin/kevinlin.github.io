Why this isn't a desktop-app MAU chart

The Claude desktop app shipped on 31 October 2024, sixteen months before the Codex desktop app on 2 February 2026 — but Anthropic has never broken out desktop usage, and OpenAI's Codex figures cover every surface at once: CLI, cloud, IDE extension and the desktop app together. There is no month-1-onward MAU series for either app to plot.

Three things that distort the comparison

  • Different starting lines. Claude Code's month 16 is May 2026; Codex's month 16 is July 2026. The same x-position is two months apart in real time.
  • Codex's July spike is partly definitional. On 9 July 2026 Codex merged into the ChatGPT desktop app, widening who counts as a Codex user.
  • Codex is no longer developer-only. OpenAI says knowledge workers are about 20% of users and growing three times faster than developers.

Numbers to treat with suspicion

Aggregator sites quote "4.2M Claude Code WAU in Q1 2026" with no source, and several attribute Codex's 1.6M February figure to Claude Code. Claude's headline totals — 245M, 56M, 18.9M — are different metrics for different surfaces from different vendors, and are not comparable to anything here.

Compiled 12 August 2026. Published figures come from OpenAI announcements and newsroom posts, Anthropic's Series G announcement, and press reporting of executive statements. Estimated points are interpolations between published figures or are derived from published growth multiples and run-rate revenue; they carry real uncertainty and should not be quoted as fact.
