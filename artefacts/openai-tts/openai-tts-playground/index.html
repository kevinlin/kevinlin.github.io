How to read the notes. OpenAI names its voices but does not describe them, so the notes on each card come from people who have listened closely: mostly a community developer who catalogued them for a voice-acting app, plus third-party samplers. Treat them as a starting point. The nine older voices have a settled character. On gpt-4o-mini-tts the instructions box can pull any voice a long way from its default, and nova, sage, shimmer and verse follow direction most readily.

Every voice here is AI-generated, not a human recording. Each play of a new voice or new settings is one billed call to /v1/audio/speech; replays come from memory. Streamed takes download as WAV, the rest as MP3. Sources: OpenAI text-to-speech guide, community listening notes, OpenAI on marin and cedar.
