One hundred years before Breath of the Wild, the Great Calamity ended in defeat. In this film it doesn't. Link, Zelda and the four Champions face Calamity Ganon together, and this time they win.

It costs them. The four Divine Beasts are destroyed, Fort Hateno falls with its soldiers, and Daruk keeps a scar. But Mipha, Daruk, Revali and Urbosa all walk out alive, and Hyrule gets the peace the game never showed: a celebration at the rebuilt castle, and Zelda finally free of her duty.

How it was made

Every shot is AI-generated, styled after the game's memory cutscenes. Keyframes were drawn with Nano Banana Pro and Nano Banana 2.1 on Fal, from 3D character sheets, and animated with MiniMax H3 at 768p, then upscaled to 1080p in the edit. Voices, sound effects and the original score are ElevenLabs. Editing and mixing run on a Python pipeline.

旷野之息正篇里，林克倒下，四位英杰死在神兽里，塞尔达独自封印了盖侬一百年。这条 IF 线里，林克、塞尔达和四位英杰一起击败了灾厄盖侬。

胜利是有代价的：四神兽全部被毁，哈特诺堡垒陷落，守军无一生还，达尔克尔留下了一道伤疤。但英杰们都活了下来。最后是重建中的海拉鲁城下的庆典，塞尔达终于不再是背负使命的公主。

制作

所有镜头均由 AI 生成，画面按游戏里的回忆过场动画来做。关键帧用 Fal 上的 Nano Banana Pro 和 Nano Banana 2.1 以 3D 角色设定图为参考绘制，再用 MiniMax H3 以 768p 生成动画，剪辑时放大到 1080p。日语配音、音效和原创配乐由 ElevenLabs 生成，中文字幕为自制。剪辑和混音用自己写的 Python 流程。
