Strike

The level the final fixing is measured against if the barrier has been breached. Below it, the client is delivered the loss one-for-one: redemption is final level divided by strike. A strike set under 100% is a discount that cushions the first leg down.

Knock-in barrier

The short put is dormant until the underlying touches this level. Continuous observation watches every fixing over the life; European observation looks only at the final fixing, which is materially friendlier and prices as a lower coupon.

Knock-out trigger

On each observation date, a fixing at or above the trigger redeems the note early at par plus coupons accrued. Good for capital, bad for carry: the client is handed cash back into the same market that just rallied, and has to reinvest.

Illustrative only. Paths are geometric Brownian motion under a drift you choose, not a risk-neutral pricing model, so the coupon shown is an input rather than a fair value.
