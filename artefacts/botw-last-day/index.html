Synopsis

Zelda's seventeenth birthday, the day the Great Calamity returned. Breath of the Wild only shows it in scattered memories. This film plays it from morning to night.

The prayer at the Spring of Wisdom fails again. The Champions say goodbye on the road, not knowing it is the last time. Then the sky turns red: the Guardians turn on Hyrule, the Divine Beasts are taken, and Mipha, Daruk, Revali and Urbosa fall one by one, each leaving their power behind for Link. At Fort Hateno, Link fights until he can't stand, and Zelda's power wakes at last, too late to save him from the wounds. The Sheikah carry him to the Shrine of Resurrection, and Zelda goes to face Calamity Ganon alone. A hundred years of sleep for him, a hundred years of holding Ganon back for her.

Made to look like the game's own memory cutscenes.

How it was made

Every shot is AI-generated. Keyframes: Nano Banana 2.1 (via Fal), drawn from 3D character sheets. Animation: MiniMax H3. Voices, sound effects and the original score: ElevenLabs. Editing and mixing: a custom Python pipeline.

简介

塞尔达十七岁生日那天，也是大灾厄复活的那一天。旷野之息里，这一天只零散地出现在回忆里；这部短片把它从早到晚完整演了一遍。

智慧之泉的祈祷又一次落空。英杰们在路口挥手告别，谁也不知道这是最后一面。随后天空染红：守护者倒戈，神兽被夺，米法、达尔克尔、力巴尔、乌尔波扎相继倒下，每个人都把最后的力量留给了林克。哈特诺堡垒前，林克战到站不起来，塞尔达的力量终于觉醒，却没能治好他的伤。希卡族把林克送进复苏神庙，塞尔达独自走向灾厄盖侬。他沉睡一百年，她守住一百年。

画面风格按游戏里的回忆过场动画来做。日语配音，中文字幕。
