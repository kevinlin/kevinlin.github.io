Why this isn't a desktop-app MAU chart

The Claude desktop app shipped on 31 October 2024, sixteen months before the Codex desktop app on 2 February 2026 — but Anthropic has never broken out desktop usage, and OpenAI's Codex figures cover every surface at once: CLI, cloud, IDE extension and the desktop app together. There is no month-1-onward MAU series for either app to plot.

Three things that distort the comparison

  • Different starting lines. Claude Code's month 16 is May 2026; Codex's month 16 is July 2026. The same x-position is two months apart in real time.
  • From July, Codex stops being one product. ChatGPT Work launched on 9 July 2026, the same day GPT-5.6 Sol went GA, and OpenAI began quoting the two agents as a single figure — 10M combined on 21 July, per Bloomberg. Only the 12 July 6M count is Codex on its own.
  • Both sides are shedding the developer-only frame. OpenAI says knowledge workers are about 20% of Codex users and the fastest-growing slice; Anthropic launched Cowork in January 2026 to carry the same non-coding demand away from Claude Code.

Numbers to treat with suspicion

  • Codex "15M", 13 August. One OpenAI engineer's post on X, no methodology, no statement of whether it covers Codex alone or Codex plus ChatGPT Work. It is plotted, as an estimate, only because it is the sole August datum that exists.
  • "4.2M Claude Code WAU in Q1 2026". Quoted by aggregators with no source. Several also attribute Codex's 1.6M February figure to Claude Code.
  • Anthropic's $47B and $65B run-rates. Company-wide, not Claude Code. The last Claude Code-specific run-rate is $2.5B, from February 2026.
  • Claude's headline totals — 245M, 56M, 18.9M. Different metrics for different surfaces from different vendors. Not comparable to anything here.
