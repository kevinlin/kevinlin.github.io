Score vs. Cost per Task

What one full run of the index costs at each reasoning-effort setting, plotted against the score it buys. Cost axis is logarithmic: each step right is roughly a doubling.

    Data table

    Source: Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.2, retrieved 5 September 2026. Scores are not comparable with earlier versions of this chart: v4.2 rescored the whole field and added two evals, which moved every model down by roughly eight to nine points and up in cost. Cost per task is the provider’s list price for the tokens one index task consumes, including cached input. Solid lines connect the same model at different reasoning-effort settings. DeepSeek’s peak / off-peak pricing is in effect, so each dashed DeepSeek line spans the two rates for one model: peak hours are 01:00–04:00 and 06:00–10:00 UTC, and every off-peak rate is half its peak counterpart. Artificial Analysis quotes the peak rate only — its published figure is the right-hand point of each pair, $0.333 per task for Pro and $0.140 for Flash. Shaded circles mark the most congested parts of the field: neighbourhoods where at least four different models sit within one nearest-neighbour gap of each other. Each caption gives the points inside its ring and how many distinct models they come from, so an effort ladder does not read as a crowd. Congestion is measured on a fixed 1440 by 900 reference plot, so the rings do not shift with the window. Use the legend to isolate a lab: hover to preview, click to keep it, click again to release. Several can be held at once. Models absent here have a v4.2 score but no published v4.2 cost measurement yet — Claude Fable 5.1's low, medium, high and xhigh rungs, the whole GPT-5.5 ladder, Claude Opus 4.8 and 4.7, GLM-5.2, Qwen3.7 Max, Gemini 3.7 and 3.8 Flash at low and medium, GPT-5.6 Terra (medium) and GPT-5.6 Luna (high).
