Scope
Claude entries start at Claude 3 and exclude the Haiku tier. GPT entries start at GPT‑4o and exclude mini, nano, and the o‑series reasoning models. Dates are announcement dates, which occasionally run ahead of general availability.

Two asterisks
GPT‑5.3 shipped only as GPT‑5.3‑Codex on 5 Feb 2026. GPT‑5.6 went to a limited government-approved preview on 26 Jun 2026 and public on 9 Jul 2026; the public date is plotted. Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 launched 9 Jun 2026, were suspended 12 Jun under a US export-control directive, and returned 1 Jul.
