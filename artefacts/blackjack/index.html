Rule 01

Beat the dealer, not the table

Every player has their own contest with the dealer. Other players' hands don't affect yours. A hand ends in one of three ways.

You win

Closer to 21

Your total is higher than the dealer's, or the dealer goes over 21 while you are still in the hand.

You lose

Bust or fall short

You go over 21, which loses at once, or the dealer finishes with a higher total than yours.

Push

Same total

You and the dealer tie. Nobody wins and your bet comes back to you.

Rule 02

What the cards are worth

Suits mean nothing. Only the rank counts, and the ace is the one card that can change its value. Build a hand to see how the total moves.

2 to 10
Worth the number on the card.
J, Q, K
Each worth 10.
Ace
Worth 11, or 1 if 11 would put you over 21.
Soft hand
Has an ace counted as 11. It can't bust on the next card.
Hard hand
No ace, or every ace counted as 1.
Blackjack
An ace and a ten-value card on the first two cards.

Rule 03

A round has five beats

The order never changes: bet, deal, players act, dealer acts, bets are settled. Step through one hand.

Rule 04

Six things you can do

Hit and stand are always there. The other four are only on offer at certain moments, mostly on your first two cards.

Rule 05

The dealer has no choices

The dealer follows one fixed rule: draw until the total is 17 or more, then stop. The only thing that differs between casinos is what happens on a soft 17.
